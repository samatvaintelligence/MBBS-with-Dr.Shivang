Common Questions

Is the degree NMC recognized? expand_more
Yes — Chuvash and the other supported universities are recognized by the National Medical Commission (NMC) and WHO. You're eligible to sit FMGE/NExT to practice in India.
Is the course taught in English? expand_more
Yes — the full 6-year MBBS is in English for international students. Russian is taught alongside as a subject so you can talk to patients during clinical years.
When do sessions start? expand_more
The academic session begins September/October. Start your application by May-June to get documents, visa, and travel sorted in time.
What about FMGE / NExT prep? expand_more
FMGE/NExT preparation is built into the mentorship — Dr. Shivang has been through it himself and guides you on coaching, mock-test cycles, and timing from your 3rd year onward.
